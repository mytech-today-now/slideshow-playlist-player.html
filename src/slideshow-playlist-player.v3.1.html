Welcome to Blend • player.html

Two independent layers. A Playlist for your audio foundation + a Slideshow for visuals. Blend them live with the opacity slider.

Everything is private and local. Use Add Files on mobile, or Add Folder on browsers that support folder access.

Pro tip: press C or tap ☰ to edit while playback continues. Swipe left/right on the stage to move through media.
